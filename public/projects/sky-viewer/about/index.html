About Sky Viewer

I designed Sky Viewer with my grandad. He observes with binoculars and a small telescope, and he was its first test user. The aim was an app that feels like going out with someone who knows the sky: it tells you what's worth looking at tonight, where to find it, and what you'll honestly see.

Most stargazing apps are either pretty and shallow or powerful and dense. This one tries to give plain answers backed by real maths: positions checked against NASA and the US Naval Observatory, cloud from a live forecast, and a recommendation engine that knows what your binoculars or telescope can show from your sky.

Lewis Wilson

Grandad's tips

The tips on object pages are written in his voice and marked as drafts until he has read them and put them right.

Red mode and accessibility

Day and Night modes aim for WCAG AA contrast. Red mode deliberately does not: it uses only dim red on black to protect your night vision at the eyepiece, which means lower contrast. Large text is in More.
