About

Mandate is a theoretical insurance product and pricing model by Lewis Wilson, who studies mathematics and economics. It is an actuarial thought experiment: a way to ask what it would take to insure the mistakes of AI agents that make payments, worked through in the open.

Why this project

AI agents can now pay for things, and nobody has settled who carries the loss when they get it wrong. That is a question about law, economics and probability at once. Building a product to answer it, and pricing that product with every assumption visible, felt like the most honest way to show how those three fit together.

The model is deliberately simple and fully inspectable. The numbers are illustrative; the structure is the contribution. If you work in insurance, payments or AI and see something wrong, that is exactly the kind of critique the project is for.

What it is not

Mandate is not a real insurer or product. There are no quotes, no applications and no advice, and the site collects no personal data. Legal summaries are sourced but are not legal advice.
