Cost model

What a patch might cost to make, and what a dose at home might cost against a clinic visit. Every patch number is an estimate from the concept brief.

How to read it

The component ranges come from the concept brief, at two production volumes[25]. The biggest uncertainty is drug loading, which depends entirely on the allergen product; the slider spans the brief's range. The scanner is reusable, so its price is spread across the doses it serves.

The clinic comparator defaults to £190, the NHS average for a consultant-led outpatient appointment in 2022/23[24]. A SCIT visit may cost more or less than this average, and it also costs the patient about 90 minutes[3], which no tariff counts.

What is not in the model. Clinical trials, regulatory approval as a combination product with medical device software, sterile manufacturing at scale, distribution, training and support. Any of these could dominate the real cost. If the estimates hold, materials need not be the main barrier; that does not mean the product would be cheap.
