About

An independent project

PitchMetrics is built by Lewis Wilson. It asks what really shapes Premier League results, answers with a design fixed in advance, and keeps a public record of every forecast it makes.

It is not affiliated with the Premier League, any club, any bookmaker or any data provider. Club names are used to identify teams only.

What this site will never do

  • Give betting advice, tips or picks. Forecasts are probabilities, published for transparency.
  • Show bookmaker prices before a match, or link to a bookmaker.
  • Edit a prediction after it is locked.

If gambling is causing you problems, free confidential support is available from the National Gambling Helpline on 0808 8020 133 or at GamCare.
