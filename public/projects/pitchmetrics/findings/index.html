Confirmatory results, test seasons 2022/23 to 2025/26

Findings

Each question was tested on top of a model of team strength, with the verdict rule fixed in advance and a correction for testing nine things at once. "No evidence" is a result, not a gap.

Form

No evidence

Does recent form predict results once you know how good a team is?

No evidence. A team in better form by one standard deviation (0.98 points per game over the last five) gains +0.03 expected points per match beyond its strength. Over a season that is between -1.5 and +3.4 points.

Rest

No evidence

Does having more rest than the opponent help?

No evidence. One standard deviation more rest (1.8 days) is worth +0.00 expected points per match.

Midweek Europe

No evidence

Does a European match in the previous five days hurt?

No evidence. The estimate points the expected way (-0.03 points per match per standard deviation) but is well within the noise.

Travel

No evidence

Do longer away trips help the home side?

No evidence after correction. Each extra 110 km of travel for the away side is worth +0.06 points per match to the home side (unadjusted p = 0.05, 0.32 after correction).

Weather and goals

No evidence

Do temperature, rain or wind change the number of goals?

No evidence. The largest weather effect is -0.01 goals per match per standard deviation, against an average of 2.95 goals.

Weather and who wins

No evidence

Does rain or wind level the field between favourite and underdog?

No evidence after correction. Heavier rain moved -0.08 expected points per match away from the favourite per standard deviation (unadjusted p = 0.05, 0.32 after correction).

Money

Not run

Does wage spending explain most of the variation in league points?

Not run yet. There is no openly licensed wage data; the test runs as soon as figures from club accounts are compiled.

Home advantage

Real effect

Is there still a home advantage?

Yes, a real effect. Home sides outscore away sides by 0.31 goals per match (0.25 to 0.37) in matches with crowds.

Is home advantage fading?

No evidence

Has home advantage declined since 2014/15?

No evidence. The trend is -0.11 goals per decade (-0.26 to +0.05): consistent with a modest decline, and with none.

Side finding

Empty stadiums

Without crowds, home sides scored 1.06 times their away rate, against 1.19 with crowds. The point estimate says about 64% of the home goal advantage went with the fans, but the interval includes no change.

Exploratory, not confirmed

Other things we looked at

New manager bounce

Teams took 0.64 points per game in the five matches before a sacking and 1.19 after. Against the strength model the five after were +0.04 points per match, not a real change: the bounce is mostly a bad run ending.

Kick-off time

Evening kick-offs showed +0.10 home points per match against afternoon games (joint test p = 0.10). Exploratory and uncorrected.

Referees

No evidence that referees differ in home results (joint p = 0.82, 28 referees).

After international breaks

No evidence of a home or favourite effect in the first round after a break (541 matches).
