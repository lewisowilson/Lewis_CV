Q4, confirmatory

Money

Does wage spending explain most of the variation in league points?

Not run

What fans assume

Football is a wages league: pay the most, finish the highest. Most fans would bet their season ticket on it.

The test, as registered

The pre-registered test: league points on log wage bill relative to the league average, needing R-squared of at least 0.5. Wage bills come from club accounts, published months after each season, so only completed seasons count.

Why it has not run

There is no free, openly licensed table of Premier League wage bills. The figures sit in each club's accounts at Companies House and have to be compiled by hand. The code is written and waiting; until the data are in, this question is reported as not run, never estimated.

The verdict

Not run

Not run yet. There is no openly licensed wage data; the test runs as soon as figures from club accounts are compiled.

Verdict rule: "Real effect" needs a Holm-adjusted p below 0.05 and a practically meaningful size; "Small effect" is detectable but small; "No evidence" means the adjusted p is 0.05 or more. Methodology.
