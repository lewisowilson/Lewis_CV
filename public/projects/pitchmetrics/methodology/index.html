How it works

Methodology

Pre-registration

Before any test-season data were analysed, the questions, outcomes, data split, models, thresholds and verdict rule were written down and committed to the repository. That commit is the proof of timing:

PREREGISTRATION.md · commit 2508ec73ce48eefdb3c636ab04a3277a714026a1 · 2026-10-25T23:20:42Z

Anything changed afterwards is listed as a deviation, with a date and a reason. There are none.

Data split

  • 2010/11 to 2013/14: Elo warm-up only, never analysed.
  • 2014/15 to 2021/22: training. Every model setting was chosen here.
  • 2022/23 to 2025/26: test. Used once, with everything frozen.
  • 2026/27: live predictions only.

Models

Elo ratings with home advantage and a margin-of-victory multiplier, mapped to home, draw and away probabilities by an ordered logit. Dixon-Coles: a Poisson model of goals with attack and defence strengths, a low-score correction and time decay, refitted every week on the previous three years. The baseline blends the two. Closing odds are turned into probabilities with Shin's method, which removes the bookmaker margin and the favourite-longshot bias, and are used only as a benchmark and a control. They are never shown before a match.

The pre-registered bar for "competitive with the market" was a gap of at most 0.010. The baseline missed it: the gap is 0.021 (95% interval 0.013 to 0.029).

Testing a factor

Each factor is added on top of the baseline's probabilities, so it only gets credit for what the baseline did not already know. Effects are reported in expected points per match for a one standard deviation change. With nine questions tested, the Holm correction stops one lucky result from being called a finding. The same tests are repeated with the closing market as the baseline, as a tougher control.

Locked predictions

Every prediction is written to a file at least 24 hours before kick-off, with a SHA-256 hash of its contents, recorded in an append-only ledger and committed to git. Locked files are never edited or deleted. A rescheduled match gets a new prediction, marked as such, and the old one stays.

Data sources

  • Results and match data: football-data.co.uk
  • Fixtures: openfootball (CC0)
  • Weather: Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0)
  • Stadium locations: Wikidata (CC0)
  • Managerial changes: Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0)

No expected goals (xG): the usual free source does not allow automated collection, so shots on target are used instead. Raw bookmaker odds are not republished here.
